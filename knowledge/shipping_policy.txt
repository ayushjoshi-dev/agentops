SHIPPING POLICY — ShopEase E-Commerce
========================================
Document Type: Company Policy
Section: Shipping Policy
Version: 2.3
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. SHIPPING PARTNERS
---------------------
ShopEase ships with the following courier partners:
- BlueDart (Premium/Express)
- Delhivery (Standard)
- Ekart (Standard/Economy)
- India Post (Economy/Remote areas)

2. DELIVERY TIMEFRAMES
------------------------

Standard Delivery (Free):
- Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata): 3-5 business days
- Tier-2 cities: 5-7 business days
- Tier-3 cities and rural areas: 7-10 business days

Express Delivery (₹149):
- Metro cities: 1-2 business days
- Other cities: 2-3 business days
Note: Express delivery not available in all pin codes.

Same-Day Delivery (₹249):
- Available in select metro cities only.
- Order must be placed before 12:00 PM.

3. ORDER TRACKING
------------------
Every order is assigned a tracking number after dispatch.
Customers can track their order by:
- Asking the AI agent: "Where is my order ORD-XXXX?" or "Track order ORD-XXXX"
- Visiting the courier website and entering the tracking number.
- Checking the "My Orders" section after login.

Typical tracking updates:
- Order Placed → Processing → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered

4. SHIPPING CHARGES
--------------------
Free Shipping: Orders above ₹499.
Below ₹499: Flat ₹49 shipping fee.
Express Delivery: ₹149 additional.
Same-Day Delivery: ₹249 additional.
Cash on Delivery: ₹25 additional handling fee.

5. DELIVERY ATTEMPTS
---------------------
Our courier partners make 3 delivery attempts:
- Attempt 1: As scheduled.
- Attempt 2: Next business day.
- Attempt 3: Third business day.

After 3 failed attempts, the package is held at the nearest courier facility for 5 days.
After 5 days, the package is returned to ShopEase and the order is marked as "Return to Origin" (RTO).

For RTO orders, a refund is issued after deducting shipping charges.

6. DELIVERY DELAYS
-------------------
Common reasons for delays:
- Natural disasters or extreme weather.
- Public holidays and festivals.
- Address verification issues.
- Remote location.
- High volume periods (festive seasons).

If your order is delayed beyond the expected date, please contact us via the AI agent or email shipping@shopease.com.

7. DAMAGED IN TRANSIT
----------------------
If your product arrived damaged:
- Do NOT accept a delivery that is visibly and heavily damaged externally.
- If you accepted a damaged delivery, contact us within 24 hours.
- Provide photos of the damaged package and product.
- We will arrange a pickup and replacement or refund.

8. ADDRESS CHANGES
-------------------
Address changes can be made ONLY if the order has not been shipped yet.
Contact the AI agent immediately to request an address change.
Once an order is marked "Shipped", addresses cannot be modified.

9. INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING
--------------------------
Currently, ShopEase ships ONLY within India.
International shipping is planned for future expansion.

10. PIN CODE AVAILABILITY
--------------------------
Not all products are available for delivery to all pin codes.
Check product availability during checkout by entering your pin code.

For shipping inquiries, contact shipping@shopease.com or chat with our AI agent.
