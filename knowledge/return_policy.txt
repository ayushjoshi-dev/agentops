RETURN POLICY — ShopEase E-Commerce
======================================
Document Type: Company Policy
Section: Return Policy
Version: 1.8
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. RETURN WINDOW
-----------------
Standard Return Window: 7 days from the date of delivery.
Extended Return Window (Electronics): 14 days from the date of delivery.
Extended Return Window (Holiday Season Dec 1–Jan 15): 21 days from date of purchase.

2. RETURN CONDITIONS
---------------------
To qualify for a return, the product must:
- Be in its original packaging.
- Be unused and in the same condition it was received.
- Include all original accessories, manuals, and tags.
- Have the original invoice or order number.

3. NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS
------------------------
The following cannot be returned:
- Opened software, video games, or digital downloads.
- Intimate apparel or swimwear for hygiene reasons.
- Customized or personalized products.
- Hazardous materials or flammable liquids.
- Any product explicitly marked "Non-Returnable" at time of purchase.
- Products with removed or tampered serial numbers.

4. HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN
-----------------------------
Step 1: Request a return through the AI chat agent or support portal.
Step 2: Provide your order ID and reason for return.
Step 3: We will email a prepaid return shipping label within 24 hours.
Step 4: Pack the product securely using the original packaging.
Step 5: Drop off at any authorized courier location.

5. RETURN SHIPPING
-------------------
ShopEase covers return shipping for:
- Defective products.
- Wrong items received.
- Products damaged during original delivery.

Customer pays return shipping for:
- Change of mind returns.
- Ordered by mistake.
- Size or color preference change.

Return shipping cost: Deducted from refund amount. Typical cost ₹80–₹150.

6. AFTER RETURN IS RECEIVED
-----------------------------
Once we receive and inspect the returned product:
- Inspection time: 1-2 business days.
- If approved: Refund processed in 5-7 business days.
- If rejected (product condition not acceptable): Product shipped back to customer.

7. EXCHANGE POLICY
-------------------
We offer direct exchanges for:
- Wrong size received.
- Wrong color received.
- Defective product replacement.

To request an exchange, ask the AI agent: "Exchange order [ORDER_ID]" or create a support ticket.

8. ELECTRONICS RETURN GUIDELINES
----------------------------------
For electronics returns:
- All data must be erased before return.
- Original charger, cables, and accessories must be included.
- Original box must be intact.
- Factory reset must be performed on mobile devices.

Questions? Contact us at returns@shopease.com or chat with our AI agent.
