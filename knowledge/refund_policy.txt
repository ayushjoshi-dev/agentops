REFUND POLICY — ShopEase E-Commerce
======================================
Document Type: Company Policy
Section: Refund Policy
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. ELIGIBILITY FOR REFUNDS
---------------------------
Customers are eligible for a full refund if:
- The refund request is made within 7 days of delivery.
- The product is defective, damaged during shipping, or different from what was ordered.
- The order was cancelled before it was shipped.

Customers are NOT eligible for a refund if:
- The product was used and is not defective.
- The refund request is made after 7 days of delivery.
- The product was damaged by the customer after delivery.
- Digital products or software licenses that have been activated.

2. REFUND PROCESS
------------------
Step 1: Customer initiates a refund request through the support portal or by contacting support.
Step 2: Customer must provide the order ID and reason for refund.
Step 3: Our team reviews the request within 2 business days.
Step 4: If approved, the refund is processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

3. RESTOCKING FEE
------------------
A restocking fee of 10% may be charged for:
- Products that are returned without defect.
- Products returned after 3 days of delivery but within the 7-day window.

No restocking fee is charged for:
- Defective or damaged products.
- Products received different from what was ordered.
- Orders cancelled before shipping.

4. PARTIAL REFUNDS
-------------------
Partial refunds may be issued if:
- Only part of the order was damaged.
- The customer returns only some items from a multi-item order.

5. NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS
------------------------
The following cannot be refunded:
- Gift cards and store credit.
- Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable".
- Personalized or custom-made products.
- Perishable goods.
- Software licenses after activation.

6. HOW TO INITIATE A REFUND
-----------------------------
Option A: Through the web chat — Ask our AI agent to "initiate a refund for order [ORDER_ID]".
Option B: Create a support ticket with priority "HIGH" and subject "Refund Request".
Option C: Email support@shopease.com with your order details.

7. REFUND STATUS
-----------------
Customers can check refund status by asking the agent: "What is the status of my refund for order [ORDER_ID]?"

8. DAMAGED PRODUCTS
--------------------
If your product arrived damaged:
- Take a photo of the damaged product and packaging.
- Contact support immediately — do not discard packaging.
- We will arrange a pickup and issue a full refund including shipping charges.
- Processing time: 3-5 business days after product pickup.

For questions, contact support@shopease.com or use our web chat.
