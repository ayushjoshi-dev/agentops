WARRANTY POLICY — ShopEase E-Commerce
========================================
Document Type: Company Policy
Section: Warranty Policy
Version: 1.5
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. WARRANTY COVERAGE
---------------------
ShopEase provides warranty coverage as follows:

Electronics (Mobiles, Laptops, Tablets): 1 year manufacturer warranty
Home Appliances (Large): 2 years manufacturer warranty + 1 year extended from ShopEase
Home Appliances (Small): 1 year manufacturer warranty
Audio Accessories (Earphones, Headphones): 6 months manufacturer warranty
Clothing & Fashion: 30 days against manufacturing defects only
Sports Equipment: 6 months
Furniture: 1 year against structural defects

Note: Warranty periods start from the date of delivery.

2. WHAT IS COVERED
-------------------
The warranty covers:
- Manufacturing defects.
- Hardware failures under normal use conditions.
- Component failures within the warranty period.
- Dead pixels on screens (more than 5 dead pixels).

3. WHAT IS NOT COVERED
-----------------------
The warranty does NOT cover:
- Physical damage caused by the customer (drops, cracks, liquid damage).
- Normal wear and tear.
- Damage caused by unauthorized repair or modification.
- Damage caused by using incompatible accessories or chargers.
- Cosmetic damage that doesn't affect product functionality.
- Damage due to acts of nature (flood, fire, lightning).
- Products with tampered or removed serial numbers.
- Software issues or virus damage.

4. HOW TO CLAIM WARRANTY
--------------------------
Step 1: Contact our support team via AI agent or email warranty@shopease.com.
Step 2: Provide your order ID, product serial number, and description of the issue.
Step 3: Our team will assess whether the issue falls under warranty.
Step 4: If approved:
  - In-home repair for large appliances (if applicable).
  - Ship-back for repair (ShopEase covers shipping for warranty claims).
  - Product replacement if repair is not possible.

Processing time: 7-14 business days for warranty claims.

5. WARRANTY CLAIM ELIGIBILITY CHECK
-------------------------------------
To check if your product is under warranty, ask the AI agent:
"Is product [PRODUCT_NAME] in order [ORDER_ID] still under warranty?"

The agent will check:
- Date of delivery.
- Product category.
- Warranty period for that category.
- Whether the product is within the warranty window.

6. EXTENDED WARRANTY
---------------------
ShopEase offers extended warranty plans:
- 1-Year Extension: 5% of product price.
- 2-Year Extension: 8% of product price.

Extended warranty can be purchased within 30 days of delivery.
Not available for products already out of warranty.

7. MANUFACTURER vs SHOPEASE WARRANTY
--------------------------------------
Some products come with both:
- Manufacturer warranty: Handled by the brand's service center.
- ShopEase warranty: Handled by ShopEase directly.

For manufacturer warranty claims, we help connect you with the brand's authorized service center.
For ShopEase warranty claims, we handle the process end-to-end.

8. PROOF OF PURCHASE
---------------------
Always keep your order ID and delivery confirmation as proof of purchase.
Warranty claims without proof of purchase may be rejected.

For warranty inquiries: warranty@shopease.com or chat with our AI agent.
