CANCELLATION POLICY — ShopEase E-Commerce
==========================================
Document Type: Company Policy
Section: Cancellation Policy
Version: 1.6
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. CANCELLATION WINDOW
------------------------
Orders can be cancelled:
- Anytime BEFORE the order is shipped.
- AFTER shipping: Orders cannot be cancelled (only returns/refunds apply after delivery).

Order statuses where cancellation is allowed:
- PLACED
- PROCESSING

Order statuses where cancellation is NOT allowed:
- SHIPPED
- OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
- DELIVERED

2. HOW TO CANCEL AN ORDER
---------------------------
Method A: Through the AI Agent
  Say: "Cancel my order ORD-XXXX"
  The agent will:
  1. Verify the order exists and belongs to you.
  2. Check if the order is still cancellable.
  3. Ask for your confirmation before cancelling.
  4. Process the cancellation upon your confirmation.

Method B: Through the Support Portal
  Go to My Orders → Select Order → Click "Cancel Order"

Method C: Through Customer Support
  Contact support@shopease.com or call 1800-XXX-XXXX.

IMPORTANT: Cancellation is IRREVERSIBLE. Once confirmed, the order cannot be restored.

3. CANCELLATION CONFIRMATION
------------------------------
Our AI agent will ALWAYS ask for confirmation before cancelling an order.
This is a safety measure — the agent never cancels without explicit user agreement.

Example:
  User: "Cancel order ORD-1025."
  Agent: "I found your order ORD-1025 for a Dell Laptop worth ₹55,000. 
         It is currently in PROCESSING status. 
         Cancelling this order is irreversible. 
         Are you sure you want to cancel? (Yes/No)"

4. REFUND AFTER CANCELLATION
------------------------------
Upon successful cancellation:
- If paid online: Full refund within 5-7 business days to original payment method.
- If Cash on Delivery: No charge is made since payment wasn't collected.
- No restocking fee for cancellations before shipping.

5. PARTIAL CANCELLATION
------------------------
For multi-item orders:
- Individual items can be cancelled as long as those specific items are not shipped.
- If some items are shipped and some are not, only unshipped items can be cancelled.

6. SELLER-CANCELLED ORDERS
----------------------------
Occasionally, ShopEase or a seller may cancel an order due to:
- Product out of stock.
- Unable to deliver to pin code.
- Payment verification failure.
- Fraudulent order detection.

In these cases:
- Customer is notified immediately by email.
- Full refund is processed automatically within 5-7 business days.

7. SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES
--------------------------
Pre-order cancellations: Allowed anytime before the shipping date.
Flash sale orders: Same cancellation rules apply.
Bulk orders: Contact B2B support for cancellation — standard policy may vary.

For cancellation help: support@shopease.com or chat with our AI agent.
